# Server Rules

> **Hard Light is an 18+ English-speaking medium roleplay server. Users under 18 will be banned immediately.**

Rules apply at all times, including between rounds. Rules highlighted with \* are **always enforced**. Other rules are enforced upon receiving a player report.

If you have questions, use the Admin Help menu (F1 in-game or the AHelp button in the lobby), or ask on the Discord.

> **Rule 0:** Implementation and interpretation of the rules is at the sole discretion of the Admins. Admins are held accountable through the Admin Code of Conduct.

## Rule 1 — Don't be a dick

Treat other players with basic respect. Harassment, slurs, and targeted griefing are not tolerated under any circumstances.

## Rule 2 — No metagaming

Do not use out-of-character information to influence in-character decisions. This includes:

- Reading or acting on information from streams/recordings of the current round
- Coordinating with other players outside the game (Discord, voice chat, etc.) to gain an in-game advantage
- Using alt accounts to assist yourself or circumvent bans

## Rule 3 — No metacommunication

Do not communicate in-game information to other players through outside channels during an active round. This includes sharing round type, antag identities, or player locations.

## Rule 4 — Roleplay standards

HardLight is a medium roleplay (MRP) server. You are expected to:

- Stay in character during normal gameplay
- Give your character a believable name (no memes, no slurs)
- Not abandon your role without good reason (see [Griefing](#rule-6--no-griefing))

## Rule 5 — Follow escalation guidelines

Violence must be proportional to provocation. You may not escalate to lethal force over minor slights. See [Escalation Policy](../space-law/escalation.md) for details.

## Rule 6 — No griefing

Do not intentionally ruin the round for other players. This includes but is not limited to:

- Releasing atmospherics without cause
- Deconstructing critical station infrastructure as a non-antag
- Abandoning a command role mid-round without notifying staff

## Rule 7 — Antag rules

If you receive an antagonist role, you must:

- Work toward your objectives in a way that creates engaging gameplay for others
- Not team up with other antags unless your role explicitly allows it
- Not kill players without reason — having an objective is not a blank check to murder everyone

## Rule 8 — Admin instructions are final

If an admin contacts you, respond promptly and follow their instructions. Disputing a ruling should be done through the [Appeal Process](../policies/appeal-process.md), not in-game.

## Rule 9 — No ERP

Explicit sexual content of any kind is strictly prohibited.

## Rule 10 — English only in OOC/radio

Out-of-character channels and radio must be used in English so that staff can moderate effectively.
