# Rule Enforcement

## Hardlit and Non-Hardlit Scope

Hardlit rules in [Server Rules](../rules/server-rules.md) are enforceable at all times. Non-hardlit rules are complaint-driven by default and should be elevated through clear player-facing triggers.

| Rule Category | Enforcement Basis | Trigger Standard |
|:--------------|:------------------|:-----------------|
| Hardlit | Always enforceable | Observable violation is sufficient |
| Non-hardlit | Complaint-driven | Direct complaint (ahelp/report) or ignored/refused LOOC prompt |

## Correction-First Workflow (Non-Hardlit)

For valid non-hardlit triggers, staff should use a correction-first process before punitive enforcement.

| Step | Staff Action | Expected Outcome |
|:-----|:-------------|:-----------------|
| 1 | Bwoink with clear issue statement | Player understands expectation |
| 2 | Prompt correction and confirm | Player has practical chance to adjust |
| 3 | Observe response and intent | Staff can distinguish confusion vs bad faith |
| 4 | Escalate only if refusal/repeat/bad faith | Enforcement remains proportionate |

This workflow may be shortened for severe disruption, obvious malicious conduct, or immediate harm to round integrity.

## In-Round Prompting Standard

Non-hardlit expectations such as job participation may be elevated after a clear prompt. Players may LOOC-prompt expected role behavior, and admins in-round may do so as players. If a clear prompt is ignored or refused, the matter can be treated as a rulebreak and handled administratively.

## Security and Command Standard

Security and Command are held to a higher standard under [Server Rule 10.1](../rules/server-rules.md#section-10-command-and-security-rules). Severe violations of outlined SOP expectations may be treated as Rule 10.1 issues, particularly where there is refusal to perform role duties, repeated disregard of standards, or meaningful degradation of others' round experience.

Common examples include repeated refusal by Security to investigate or arrest after clear OOC prompt, repeated command abandonment without delegation, and intentional disregard of core procedure that materially harms round quality.

## SSD Exploitation and Player Impact

Stealing from SSD players should be evaluated through [Rule 1.5](../rules/server-rules.md#section-1-behavior-rules). Staff review should center on intent, impact on enjoyment, and whether behavior stops after intervention.

## Punitive Action

For the full punitive-action escalation path and standards for `message`, `note`, and `watchlist`, see [Punitive Action](punitive-action.md).

## Whitelist Privilege and Community Fit

Playing on HardLight is a privilege. As a whitelist server, access may be revoked when a player is not a good fit for the community.

Full admins may revoke whitelist access when they determine fit is no longer appropriate. Whitelist revocation does not require ban-policy parity with external platforms. Two-admin concurrence should be used when practical, and actions should always be documented with clear internal rationale.

### Whitelist Revocation Appeal

Whitelist revocation appeals are handled through the staff ticket process. Revocation removes game-server access, but does not inherently remove Discord presence.

## Enforcement Priority

When deciding outcomes, staff should prioritize: protection of current player experience and round integrity, behavior correction through communication, proportionate escalation only where correction fails or severity warrants, and finally removal of persistent bad-fit behavior through stronger actions (including whitelist revocation where appropriate).
