# Rule Interpretation / Ruling Request

This page defines how admins should act when a situation appears to fall under a rule, but the application is not verbatim or immediately obvious.

## Core Standard

When interpretation is unclear in the moment, rule in the way that best protects community expectations and the Admin Code of Conduct mission: preserve round quality, reduce harm, and prioritize fun for the broader playerbase.

Do not default to the narrowest technical reading if that outcome clearly undermines player experience.

**This process is also how precedent is created.** Documented rulings become shared reference points that let staff enforce rules uniformly across similar incidents, rather than relying on one-off personal interpretation.

## In-Round Decision Framework

| Step | Action | Outcome |
|:-----|:-------|:--------|
| 1 | Identify the likely governing rule(s) and impact | Framing is clear and defensible |
| 2 | Apply the best-faith interpretation aligned with community standards | Immediate round harm is reduced |
| 3 | Communicate the ruling to affected players | Expectations are transparent |
| 4 | Record the action and rationale for post-round review | Future consistency is improved |

## Post-Round Ruling Request Thread

After an interpretation-based ruling, the acting admin should open a staff thread for review and deliberation by admins/directors.

Thread should include:

- What occurred (concise incident timeline)
- Which rule(s) were considered
- What ruling was applied in-round
- Why that ruling best fit community expectations and the mission of fun
- What alternatives were considered
- A direct request for admin/director feedback on whether the standard should be affirmed, refined, or changed

## Reversibility Reminder

Admins should remember that most enforcement actions are reversible.

Punitive actions taken to protect round integrity can usually be adjusted or undone after staff review if the final interpretation changes. This should reduce hesitation to act in good faith during active incidents while preserving accountability through follow-up deliberation.
