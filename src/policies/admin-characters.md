# Admin Characters

Admin characters fall into one of three categories: personal, medium impact, and high impact.

| Category | Summary |
|:---------|:--------|
| Personal | A role much like a regular player or crew member. Abilities and gear should not be modified in any way other than cosmetic, and everything they have on them should be commercially or publicly available to players. |
| Medium impact | A character involved in station events and situations, generally intended to be used as an antag, trickster, or similar role. These should stay within the normal expectations of midround threats such as dragons or revenants, and should be used in moderation. |
| High impact | A direct allegory to admins. These are generally gods or godlike characters, and should remain observers and helpers that guide the story or narrative in positive ways. |

```admonish info
Admin characters should be realistic in scope and expectation. Avoid turning them into major founders, owners, or other overblown roles that pull them out of the setting.
```

## Personal Characters

Personal characters can follow one of two design flows.

- Standard: admin characters who fill a role much like what regular players or crew would. Their abilities and gear should never be modified in any way other than cosmetic, and everything they have on them should be commercially or publicly available to players.
- Admeme: admin admeme characters function beyond the general specifics expected of player characters. These characters should generally not be used directly in regular play, but instead in personal roleplay between yourself and select consenting individuals. Self-contained stories like this are not subject to moderation.

## Medium Impact Characters

Medium impact characters are the sort of character you would see directly involved in station events and situations. They are intended to be antags, tricksters, and other such things. Generally, a medium impact admeme character will never have capabilities that go beyond the standard expectation of midround antags such as dragons or revenants.

Medium impact characters are subject to restrictions on gameplay, as not everyone wants to fight giant monsters or creatures. Use them in moderation and be keenly aware of the enjoyment players experience when interacting. These are the kind of characters we would need to quickly snuff if players ask you to stop.

## High Impact Characters

High impact characters are the direct allegories to ourselves as admins. Playing as these characters can essentially fulfill your duties as an admin while interacting and roleplaying with the round.

Generally these are gods or have godlike abilities, and are expected to avoid directly interfering in situations without player request, prayer, or similar need.

They typically would not be seen directly engaging in combat of any kind, as doing so would be akin to running around with a gib stick. They are first and foremost observers and helpers, here to interact with the round in positive ways to help guide the story or narrative.

## Example Characters

### Hardscale

Hardscale is a personal character that can be played as standard or as an admeme depending on context. His stubborn attitude makes him deny the divinity of Sotek inside him and try to do things for himself, so he generally functions similarly to a player character up until death. Unless recovered, play as or with him should largely cease during a round if he gets round removed.

As an admeme, he can call upon Sotek to change into a dragon, teleport around, and generally help save people or answer ahelps. While using Sotek's power, he only engages with non-player antags, and PvP would not be seen from him in most scenarios.

### Friend-shaped

Friend-shaped is a personal character that is a friendly Xeno Rouny who works for the FXS project. He is definitely an admeme character, having abilities far beyond what would be expected of something normal, but the character is played with heavy restrictions.

He is largely incapable of combat unless faced with creatures, xenos, or other entities; humanoids are entirely off limits for him to engage. In story, this is explained by the FXS project actively altering the amygdala of their xenos subjects to prevent them from exhibiting aggressive behavior.

### Doctor Dinosaur

Doctor Dinosaur is most definitely a medium impact character. He is generally associated with some form of event that will affect the station just by existing, though he is occasionally played as an admeme or even a standard character in round as part of his unpredictability.

Due to his nature as a medium impact character, there is always a need to be beholden to whether players enjoy having him around or associating with him at all. He is a character to play in moderation and is the most likely of the examples to stray too far, so knowing when to stop with this character is important at all times.

### Sotek

Sotek is a god and is always a high impact character. That means his play should remain restrictive, with interaction limited to helping people when asked or during times of trouble. He should be kept aware of what is happening and act as an admin would, answering prayers and ahelps in people's times of need.

He should not be played as a combat-oriented character, and he should not do battle with anyone without prior consent or setup, save perhaps for the idea of a clash between gods.

## Practical Limits

Admin characters should at no point while spawned in the round be entirely invincible. Use of godmode and similar tools should be discouraged, as it generally frustrates players.

Admin characters may be spawned with a standard loadout from the outfits tab that fits their role or position in roleplay.
