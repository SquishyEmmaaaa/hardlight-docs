# Escalation Policy

Violence in HardLight must be **proportional**. This policy applies to all players — security, crew, and antagonists alike.

## The Escalation Ladder

Responses must not skip more than one step above the aggression received. Each step up requires a corresponding trigger from the other party.

| Step | Example Actions |
|:----:|:----------------|
| 1 | Verbal argument, insults |
| 2 | Shoving, minor property damage |
| 3 | Unarmed fighting |
| 4 | Armed fighting (non-lethal weapons) |
| 5 | Armed fighting (lethal weapons) |
| 6 | Lethal force / killing |

**Example:** Someone shoves you (step 2). You may respond up to step 3 (unarmed fighting). You may **not** immediately draw a weapon.

## Exceptions

The following bypass normal escalation rules:

- **Self-defense against lethal force** — If someone is actively trying to kill you, lethal response is justified.
- **Protecting others** — You may use force proportional to the threat directed at a third party.
- **Security arrests** — Security may use non-lethal force to subdue any resisting suspect. Lethal force is only justified if the suspect is an active lethal threat.
- **Confirmed antagonists** — Once someone is confirmed as a lethal antagonist (e.g., a confirmed murderer), crew and security may use lethal force to stop them.

## Prior Escalation Carries Over (Within a Round)

If someone has already attacked you in the same round, you do not need to restart the ladder from step 1 if they approach you again with clear hostile intent.

## Antag Escalation

Antagonists may escalate more freely than regular crew, but **mass murder without narrative purpose is still against the rules**. Having an objective does not grant unlimited license to kill everyone in your path.

> **Important:** When in doubt, de-escalate. Ahelp if you believe someone is breaking escalation rules.
