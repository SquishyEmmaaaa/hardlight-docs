# Legal Procedures

Procedures that Security and Command must follow when issuing sentences, conducting lawful searches, and managing confinement.

---

## Sentencing Rules

- Sentences must match the charges (see [Crimes & Punishments](crimes-and-punishments.md)).
- Sentences may be reduced by up to 50% for cooperation or mitigating circumstances.
- Sentences may be increased by up to double for aggravating circumstances or repeat offenses.
- Multiple charges have sentences served **consecutively** unless HoS grants concurrent sentencing.

---

## Searches

| Type | Authorization Required |
|:-----|:----------------------|
| Pat-down (pockets/hands) | Probable cause or arrest |
| Full search (bag/ID/equipment) | Arrest, or reasonable suspicion of contraband |
| Area/locker search | Captain or HoS authorization |

Searches must be logged if they are contested.

---

## Permabrig

Permabrigging is reserved for capital crimes. Requirements:

- Charges must be at the capital crime level.
- Captain or HoS must authorize.
- Prisoner must be informed of their charges and that they are permanently confined.
- Prisoner retains access to a lawyer and the right to appeal.

---

```admonish important
Security is not judge, jury, and executioner. When in doubt, brig and consult the Captain or HoS.
```
