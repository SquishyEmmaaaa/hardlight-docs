# Banning Policy

Guidelines for when and how bans are issued on HardLight.

## Ban Tiers

| Tier | Duration | Typical Reason |
|:-----|:--------:|:---------------|
| Warning | — | Minor first offense; documented for future reference |
| Short ban | 1–3 days | Repeated minor violations or a single moderate violation |
| Medium ban | 3–14 days | Serious violations or repeat moderate violations |
| Long ban | 14–30 days | Severe violations or a pattern of medium-tier offenses |
| Permanent ban | Indefinite | Extreme violations, repeated evasion, or no sign of intent to improve |

## Escalating Bans

Bans generally escalate with repeat offenses. A player with a history of prior bans will receive longer bans for the same infraction than a first-time offender.

A prior ban more than **6 months** old with no further incidents may be given reduced weight at admin discretion.

## Job Bans

Job bans restrict access to specific roles (e.g., Security, Command) without removing general server access. They are issued when a player repeatedly misuses a role.

Job bans may be **permanent** for severe misuse or may have a defined duration.

## Permanent Bans

Permanent bans are reserved for:

- Extreme griefing (mass RDM, station destruction, etc.)
- Hate speech, slurs, or targeted harassment
- Sharing CSAM or illegal content
- Confirmed ban evasion (see below)
- Doxxing or threatening other players

Permanent bans require documentation and must be reviewed by a senior admin before being issued, except in cases where immediate action is necessary to protect the community.

## Ban Evasion

Playing on an alternate account while banned is ban evasion. All accounts found to be evading will be permanently banned. The original ban's duration will reset upon detection.

## Appealing a Ban

All bans except permanent bans may be appealed after serving at least half the ban duration. Permanent bans may be appealed after **30 days**. See the [Appeal Process](appeal-process) for details.
