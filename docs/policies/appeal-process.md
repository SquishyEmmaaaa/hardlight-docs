# Appeal Process

How to appeal a ban, job ban, or admin action you believe was unjust.

## Before You Appeal

- Read the [Server Rules](../rules/server-rules) and [Banning Policy](banning-policy) to understand why the action was taken.
- Wait until you are eligible to appeal (see [Banning Policy](banning-policy) for timelines).
- Approach the process in good faith. Appeals submitted in anger or containing insults will be dismissed.

## How to Submit an Appeal

Appeals are submitted through the **HardLight Discord** in the `#ban-appeals` channel or via the forum if one exists.

Your appeal must include:

1. **Your in-game name and account name**
2. **The date the ban was issued** (approximate is fine)
3. **The ban reason** as shown in-game or in the ban message
4. **Your explanation** — what happened from your perspective
5. **Why you believe the ban should be lifted or reduced**

## What Happens Next

1. A senior admin will review your appeal within **72 hours**.
2. They may ask clarifying questions — respond promptly and honestly.
3. You will be notified of the outcome in the same thread.

## Possible Outcomes

| Outcome | Meaning |
|:--------|:--------|
| Upheld | The ban stands as issued |
| Reduced | The ban duration is shortened |
| Lifted | The ban is removed |
| Conditional lift | Ban removed with conditions (e.g., probationary period) |

## What Appeals Are Not

- An opportunity to argue that the rules are wrong
- A place to attack or insult the admin who banned you
- A guarantee of reinstatement

{: .note }
If your appeal is denied, you may resubmit after **30 days** only if you have new information or circumstances have changed. Repeat appeals with the same arguments will be dismissed.

## Contesting Admin Behavior

If you believe an admin acted improperly (not just that you disagree with the outcome), you may file a **staff complaint** separately from a ban appeal. Contact a senior admin or server owner directly.
