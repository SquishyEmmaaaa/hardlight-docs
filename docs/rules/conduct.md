# Conduct & Expectations

Standards of behavior expected from all players, beyond the core rule list.

## General Conduct

- **Assume good faith.** Most mistakes are not malicious. Check before escalating.
- **Use OOC for out-of-character issues.** Don't use in-character channels to argue about game mechanics or complain about other players.
- **Ahelp, don't retaliate.** If someone is breaking the rules, use `Admin Help` (F1). Do not take matters into your own hands.

## New Player Conduct

Veteran players are encouraged to help new players learn the game. Killing or griefing new players specifically because they are new is against the rules.

## Command Conduct

Players in command roles are held to a higher standard:

- You are responsible for your department. Neglecting your role or going off doing your own thing without delegating is considered griefing.
- Do not demote or arrest crew members without valid in-character reason and (where reasonable) due process.
- Coordinate with other heads of staff regularly.

## Security Conduct

Security officers must follow [Space Law](../space-law/). Acting as an executioner, ignoring due process, or using excessive force will result in IC consequences and potentially OOC punishment.

## Antag Conduct

Antagonists are expected to create fun and interesting gameplay, not simply end rounds as fast as possible. Mass murder with no narrative reason is not acceptable antag play.

{: .warning }
"It's what my character would do" is not a valid defense for breaking conduct rules.
