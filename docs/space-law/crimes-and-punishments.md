# Crimes & Punishments

All crimes are listed with their severity tier, description, and standard sentence. Security officers should use these as guidelines — not hard rules — and apply judgment based on context.

---

## Sentence Guidelines

| Sentence Type | When to Use |
|:--------------|:------------|
| Fine | Minor infractions; first offenses |
| Brig (short) | 2–5 minutes; minor crimes |
| Brig (medium) | 5–10 minutes; moderate crimes |
| Brig (long) | 10–20 minutes; serious crimes |
| Permabrig | Permanent confinement; capital crimes without death penalty |
| Execution | Capital crimes; requires Captain or HoS authorization |

Sentences may be **reduced** for cooperation or mitigating circumstances, and **increased** for repeat offenses or aggravating factors (up to double the listed time).

---

## Tier 1 — Minor Crimes

| Crime | Description | Standard Sentence |
|:------|:------------|:-----------------:|
| Trespassing | Entering a restricted area without authorization | Fine or 2 min brig |
| Disorderly Conduct | Creating a public disturbance | Warning or fine |
| Vandalism | Damaging property without significant harm | Fine or 2 min brig |
| Petty Theft | Stealing low-value items | Fine or 3 min brig |
| Insubordination | Refusing a lawful order from a superior | Warning or 2 min brig |

---

## Tier 2 — Moderate Crimes

| Crime | Description | Standard Sentence |
|:------|:------------|:-----------------:|
| Assault | Attacking someone without causing serious injury | 5 min brig |
| Theft | Stealing significant items or equipment | 5–8 min brig |
| Breaking & Entering | Forced entry into a restricted area | 5 min brig |
| Possession of Contraband | Carrying items banned on station | 5–7 min brig + confiscation |
| Resisting Arrest | Fleeing or fighting an arresting officer | +3 min added to sentence |
| Manslaughter | Killing someone without intent (negligence) | 10 min brig |

---

## Tier 3 — Serious Crimes

| Crime | Description | Standard Sentence |
|:------|:------------|:-----------------:|
| Aggravated Assault | Assault causing serious injury or with a deadly weapon | 10 min brig |
| Grand Theft | Stealing critical equipment (weapons, IDs, engines) | 10–15 min brig |
| Sabotage | Intentionally damaging station systems | 15 min brig |
| Kidnapping | Unlawfully detaining a crew member | 15 min brig |
| Attempted Murder | Attacking someone with clear lethal intent | 15–20 min brig |

---

## Capital Crimes

Capital crimes may result in permanent confinement or, with Captain authorization, execution.

| Crime | Description | Standard Sentence |
|:------|:------------|:-----------------:|
| Murder | Intentionally killing a crew member | Permabrig or execution |
| Terrorism | Acts intended to destroy the station or cause mass casualties | Permabrig or execution |
| Grand Sabotage | Destroying critical station infrastructure | Permabrig |
| Mutiny | Organized, violent overthrow of command | Permabrig or execution |

{: .warning }
Executions require **Captain authorization** and must be logged. They should only be used when the individual poses an ongoing lethal threat that cannot be otherwise contained.

---

## Notes for Security

- Always attempt to **brig before lethal force**.
- Inform the prisoner of their charges.
- Allow the prisoner to contact a lawyer or command if requested (within reason).
- Do not hold prisoners beyond their sentence without new charges.
