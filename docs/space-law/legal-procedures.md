# Legal Procedures

Correct procedure for security when arresting, processing, and releasing crew members.

## Arrest Procedure

1. **Announce the arrest** — Verbally inform the suspect of the charges before or during detainment.
2. **Restrain if resisting** — Use cuffs only if the suspect is resisting or a flight risk.
3. **Search the suspect** — Confiscate contraband and evidence. Return non-contraband items.
4. **Transport to brig** — Do not harm the suspect in transit.
5. **Process in the brig** — Set the brig cell timer, log the charges, and inform the HoS of serious crimes.

{: .warning }
Arresting someone without stating charges and then holding them indefinitely is unlawful imprisonment and is reportable to command.

## Sentencing

- Set the cell timer to the sentence from [Crimes & Punishments](crimes-and-punishments).
- For stacked charges, add sentences together up to a **maximum of 25 minutes** for non-capital crimes.
- The Head of Security may reduce or extend sentences within reason.
- The Captain may pardon any sentence.

## Searches

- **Probable cause required** — You must have a specific reason to search a crew member.
- **Pat-down** — Allowed for anyone being arrested or anyone in a high-security area.
- **Full search** — Requires arrest or a direct order from the HoS/Captain.

## Perma-brigging

Permanent confinement requires one of the following:
- Captain authorization
- Head of Security authorization for capital crimes
- At least two heads of staff in agreement if command is compromised

Perma-prisoners must be given a cell with basic amenities (bed, toilet, access to food/water).

## Releasing Prisoners

- Release the prisoner when their sentence expires.
- Return all confiscated non-contraband items upon release.
- If the prisoner committed a crime while in custody, re-process them before release.

## Use of Force

| Situation | Permitted Force |
|:----------|:----------------|
| Suspect complying | No force required |
| Suspect fleeing | Non-lethal force to stop (taser, baton) |
| Suspect fighting back | Non-lethal preferred; lethal if life is threatened |
| Suspect armed and lethal | Lethal force authorized |
| Confirmed capital criminal | Lethal force authorized |

{: .note }
Security officers who abuse their authority (excessive force, false imprisonment, corruption) are subject to demotion and brig time themselves.
